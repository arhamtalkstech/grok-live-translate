Vantorra Key app guide

What the Vantorra Key app is
Vantorra Key is the authenticator app for Vantorra Online. It replaces the hardware token. Each user activates the app once on their own phone; after that it creates the login codes and approves payments.

Activating the Vantorra Key app on a phone
Step 1. Install Vantorra Key from the App Store or Google Play.
Step 2. Open the app and accept the terms of use.
Step 3. Choose a six-digit app PIN and enter it twice. The bank never asks for this PIN.
Step 4. On a computer, log in to Vantorra Online with your activation letter, then open Settings and choose Activate Vantorra Key. A QR code appears.
Step 5. In the app, tap Scan and scan the QR code on the computer screen.
Step 6. The app shows a six-digit confirmation code. Type it into Vantorra Online and select Confirm. Activation is complete.

Logging in to Vantorra Online with the app
Step 1. Go to the Vantorra Online login page and enter your login ID.
Step 2. Choose Log in with Vantorra Key. A QR code appears.
Step 3. Open the app, enter your app PIN, and tap Scan.
Step 4. Scan the QR code. The app shows a login code.
Step 5. Type the login code into the login page. You are logged in.

Moving the app to a new phone
Install Vantorra Key on the new phone, then ask your company administrator to reset your Vantorra Key in Access Manager. You receive a new activation letter by email within one business day and activate the app again as above. The app on the old phone stops working after the reset.

Too many wrong PIN attempts
After five wrong app PINs the app locks. Your company administrator resets it in Access Manager, and you activate the app again.
